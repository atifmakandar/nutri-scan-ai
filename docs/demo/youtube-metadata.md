## Title

**NutriScan AI — Smart Feed & Silage Quality Screening for Dairy Farmers | Buildathon Demo**

## Description

NutriScan AI turns feed and silage quality screening into a farmer-first workflow:
sample, scan, analyze, and act.

The prototype lets operators keep batch identity, measured readings, explainable
screening results, advisories, private evidence, and traceable farm history together.
It also demonstrates multilingual measurement help, offline drafts, reports, and a
reviewed path for ESP32 sensor input.

This demo intentionally shows the product's limits: the score is an uncalibrated
prototype indicator, not a laboratory result or feed-safety certificate. Production
use requires laboratory-labelled data, calibration, held-out farm testing, qualified
review, and expert validation.
