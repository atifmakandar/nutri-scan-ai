# NutriScan AI — 90-second buildathon demo

## Title card

**NutriScan AI: Smart Feed & Silage Quality Screening for Safer Dairy Farming**

## Voiceover script

Every day, dairy farmers make feed decisions with scattered readings, paper notes,
and very little traceability.

NutriScan AI turns that process into one clear field workflow: sample, scan, analyze,
and act.

A farmer creates a batch, chooses feed or silage, and records measured values from a
report, instrument, or reviewed sensor input. The guided form keeps the sample
identity, units, and ten required readings together—so missing data is visible rather
than guessed.

NutriScan then produces an explainable prototype score, risk signal, anomalies, and
the next practical action. This example is a low-risk result at 93.9 out of 100. The
important part is the context: it is explicitly uncalibrated, not a laboratory test,
and never a feed-safety certificate.

Every saved screening becomes traceable farm history. Operators can compare batches,
download a report, use multilingual measurement help, keep offline drafts, attach
private evidence photos, and connect reviewed ESP32 readings when available.

NutriScan AI does not pretend to replace a laboratory. It makes field evidence more
consistent, explainable, and actionable—while keeping the path to calibration and
expert validation clear.

## Recording direction

- Use a calm, confident voice at approximately 125–135 words per minute.
- Let the score and disclaimer remain on screen long enough to read.
- Keep the final disclaimer visible for the last two seconds.
- Do not describe photos as diagnosis or the score as certified safety.
