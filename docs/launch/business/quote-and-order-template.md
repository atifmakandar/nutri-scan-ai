## Order scope

Attach the completed [pilot proposal](pilot-proposal-template.md) with named farm,
user/batch caps, deployment/device/browser, deliverables, included hours, dates and
workflow-only [ACCEPTANCE TARGET]. Identify whether any full-screening demonstration
uses synthetic inputs or suitable real inputs; do not substitute one for the other.

The prototype does not establish nutrient prediction, lab accuracy, toxin detection,
certified calibration, diagnosis or feeding safety. Photos are evidence only; testing
requests are local records. No automatic external booking or lab interpretation.
