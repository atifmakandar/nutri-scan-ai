## Drafting instructions

1. **Parties and authority:** verify entity, addresses, signatory/payer authority,
   order of precedence, effective version and a reliable acceptance record.
2. **Use and reliance:** state uncalibrated prototype limits prominently. Do not
   promise lab accuracy, toxin detection, diagnosis, feeding clearance or guaranteed
   safety. Full screening requires ten suitable inputs; observation-only use has no
   score. Qualified advice/reference testing remains separate.
3. **Feature boundaries:** photos are evidence, not AI diagnosis; laboratory requests
   are local records, reminders are in-app, offline reload is public help only and
   draft save/load drops instrument provenance. No unsupported device compatibility.
4. **Customer duties:** authorize supplied material, assign individual users, maintain
   suitable devices/connectivity, review shared reports, protect credentials and
   report suspected exposure. Do not transfer all supplier responsibilities to users.
5. **Data/confidentiality:** agree rights and organization roles, permitted delivery
   use [DATA USE PERMISSION], actual providers, security duties, incidents and the
   [privacy notice](privacy-notice-template.md). Optional research/publicity requires
   separate permission; customer data does not become supplier-owned by hosting it.
6. **Administrative gaps:** account disabling/recovery, bulk export, farm erasure,
   backup expiry and audit operations are not complete self-service MVP features.
   Confirm a reliable, authorized manual process before promising deadlines.
7. **IP and third parties:** distinguish pre-existing software, customer material,
   generated reports, laboratory rights and commissioned work. Review dependency
   licences, third-party service terms and any hardware loan/supply separately.
8. **Fees and remedies:** agree invoicing, taxes, collection timing, disputed charges,
   change approval, cancellation/refunds and suspension/cure. No unquoted pass-throughs.
9. **Risk allocation:** counsel to draft appropriate commitments, remedies, mandatory
   rights, insurance, third-party claims and any lawful [LIABILITY CAP]. No blanket
   waiver or prototype disclaimer substitutes for responsible operation.
10. **Termination/disputes:** set notice, cure, export/access/retention handling,
    confidentiality survival, [GOVERNING LAW] and [DISPUTE PROCESS].
