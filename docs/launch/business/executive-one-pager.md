## The problem to test

Dairy teams may keep silage/feed observations, readings and reports in separate notebooks and messages, making batch follow-up difficult. Discovery must establish how often this occurs, who owns the task and what it costs; no market-size or savings claim is assumed.

## Initial customer and wedge

Start with a dairy farm managing multiple silage batches and a named operator, manager and qualified adviser/testing route. Offer **silage/feed screening workflow and traceable follow-up**, beginning with batch cards and observation-only checks when complete measurements are unavailable. Cooperatives/advisers are potential introduction channels, not confirmed customers.

## What exists today

- Farm accounts and roles; feed/silage batch records, private photos, Yes/No/Unknown observations and in-app reminders.
- Farm-local testing-request tracking and report storage; staff contact their chosen provider separately.
- Four-step screening with all ten inputs required, reviewed optional USB-serial entry, uncalibrated scoring, saved history/trends and HTML/print reports.
- English/Hindi/Marathi interface guidance, explicit local reading drafts and a public-help PWA shell with limited offline scope.

The current demo uses synthetic serial values and a separate synthetic calibration scaffold. Photos are evidence only; nutrient values are supplied inputs, not measurements produced by this demo. See the [demo script](sales-demo-script.md).

## Why this could win

A practical batch-to-follow-up workflow combines record retrieval, evidence and explicit human review. The potential defensibility is consented workflow learning, reliable operations and eventually properly governed reference data. Neither an exclusive dataset nor a validated model moat exists today.
