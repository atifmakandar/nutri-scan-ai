# Business launch checklist

> **DRAFT — legal/accounting review required.** Business readiness checklist for a bounded prototype workflow pilot. No item is pre-completed; a checked item needs dated evidence. Scientific/commercial targets are not achieved results.

For each item record [OWNER], [DUE DATE], [STATUS] and [EVIDENCE LINK] in the pilot/launch working record. Use statuses `not_started`, `in_progress`, `blocked`, `done`; `done` requires evidence and reviewer, not intention. Do not interpret this checklist as regulatory certification.

## Positioning and customer fit

- [ ] Agree the first ICP, buyer/user/payer and silage/feed workflow wedge; document discovery evidence and objections, not only positive feedback. [GTM plan](go-to-market-plan.md)
- [ ] Review the [one-pager](executive-one-pager.md), [business plan](business-plan.md), [pitch](investor-pitch-outline.md) and [FAQ](customer-faq.md) against the [claims matrix](README.md#claims-matrix).
- [ ] Remove any lab-accuracy, toxin-detection, certified-calibration, diagnosis, guaranteed-safety or automatic-lab-booking promise from customer-ready copy. Do not equate a LOW score or heuristic confidence with validated safety/accuracy.
- [ ] Verify identities and permission before using contacts, testimonials, grants or customer/partner logos; none is supplied by this pack.

## Demo and pilot operational fit

- [ ] Rehearse the [actual app/emulator script](sales-demo-script.md) on the target browser/device; label synthetic data, unknown calibration and manual fallbacks honestly.
- [ ] Show observation-only checks for missing measurements; confirm all ten suitable readings are required for full screening. No invented inputs or photo-derived diagnosis.
- [ ] Confirm local-only testing requests, in-app reminders and report-download limits with the buyer. Name the human responsible for contacting a chosen lab/adviser.
- [ ] Explain public-help PWA versus private data, explicit draft sync and loss of instrument provenance in draft save/load. Agree connectivity and manual fallback expectations.
- [ ] Review the existing [verification evidence and device gaps](../../fieldwork-verification.md). Check deployment, farm-role access, saved readback and backup/restore responsibilities on the chosen pilot setup before real data; record actual results separately from historical tests.
- [ ] Agree language/measurement guidance review by a qualified domain reviewer and a route for concerns requiring professional review.

## Commercial, accounting and legal readiness

- [ ] Complete the [proposal](pilot-proposal-template.md): dates, caps, deliverables, baseline, [ACCEPTANCE TARGET], exclusions and named operator/buyer/payer.
- [ ] Replace illustrative pricing with [PILOT PRICE] / [MONTHLY PRICE] supported by delivery assumptions. Review support hours, processing, travel, hardware/lab exclusions, fixed-cost allocations and cash timing. [Unit economics](pricing-and-unit-economics.md)
- [ ] Obtain accountant review of [TAX TREATMENT], invoice/payment handling, withholding if relevant, refunds and gross/net pass-through treatment; record [PAYMENT TERMS]. No tax status/rate is assumed.
- [ ] Have counsel turn the [agreement discussion template](pilot-agreement-template.md) into appropriate execution terms: authority, scope, acceptance, data rights, support, termination, liability, disputes and notices. Resolve [LIABILITY CAP], [GOVERNING LAW] and [DISPUTE PROCESS].
- [ ] Agree [DATA USE PERMISSION], farm-role access, external sharing, [RETENTION PERIOD] and [EXPORT WINDOW]. Training/research and branding need separate permission; manually delivered export/deletion commitments need technical confirmation.
- [ ] Set [SUPPORT HOURS], [RESPONSE TARGET], escalation contact and change-control process; distinguish response from resolution/uptime. Complete signature/payment prerequisites under reviewed terms.

## Partners and scientific boundary

- [ ] Record verified partner status in the [pipeline](../templates/business/partner-pipeline.csv); outreach/interest is not an executed relationship or accreditation.
- [ ] If laboratory collaboration is needed, settle methods, sampling/chain of custody, reports, fees, responsibility and permissions using the [MoU outline](lab-partner-mou-outline.md).
- [ ] Keep any reference study separate from workflow acceptance and pricing. Before collection, freeze intended targets, representative sampling, units/basis, immutable IDs, QC rules, grouped/time splits and acceptance thresholds with qualified owners. [Calibration protocol](../../calibration-workflow.md)
- [ ] Keep synthetic scaffold reports and coefficients out of accuracy claims. Future expanded claims need target-specific held-out evaluation, uncertainty and expert release approval; no sales deadline substitutes for validation.

## Measurement, conversion and closeout

- [ ] Complete consented [discovery logs](../templates/business/customer-discovery-log.csv); distinguish observed behavior, stated intent, signed commitments and cash received.
- [ ] Agree baseline/current task method and [KPI log](../templates/business/pilot-kpi-log.csv) denominators; exclude demo entries from adoption counts and show missing data explicitly.
- [ ] Review support load, collections, missing-input failures, task retrieval and follow-up weekly; re-scope if costs or confusion exceed the agreed boundary.
- [ ] Hold the end-of-pilot acceptance review and document issues/remedies. Seek a separate signed/paid continuation; measure conversion using mature cohorts, including terminated pilots. [Conversion definition](go-to-market-plan.md#pilot-to-paid-conversion-playbook)
- [ ] If ending, deliver the agreed manual export/access/retention closeout; document costs, reasons and lessons. No silent renewal or presumed research reuse.
