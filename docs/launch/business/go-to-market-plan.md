## ICP and buying roles

First ICP: a dairy farm actively storing silage in several identifiable batches, with recurring checks, a smartphone/browser, an operator and a manager willing to compare the current process with a six-week pilot. Prioritize a reachable cluster to control travel cost. Geography and farm-size thresholds must come from discovery.

| Role | Candidate | Qualification question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User | Feed-store operator / farmer | Who records observations, retrieves reports and completes reminders? |
| Buyer | Farm owner / manager | Who approves a new workflow and evaluates its value? |
| Payer | Farm entity; possibly a sponsoring cooperative under separate terms | Who signs, receives invoices and controls the budget? |
| Influencer | Nutrition adviser / veterinarian / testing provider | Who reviews concerns and determines appropriate confirmatory testing? |

A sponsor paying for several farms does not imply a cross-farm dashboard or shared data rights. Define each farm's account and permissions explicitly.

## Measurable sales funnel

Illustrative first eight-week outreach experiment: **40 consent-respecting contacts → 12 discovery interviews → 6 qualified opportunities → 3 pilot proposals → 2 signed/paid pilot starts**. This is a proposed activity funnel; pilots started late in this window finish later. It is not a revenue forecast. Keep unique farms/deals as denominators, not email counts.

| Stage | Exit criterion | Action / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contacted | Relevant person agrees to a conversation. | Log source, role, permission and next date. |
| Discovered | Recent problem instance and incumbent process understood. | [Interview guide](customer-discovery-interview.md); record objections and no-problem responses. |
| Qualified | Named buyer/user/payer, task frequency, data boundary, pilot dates and plausible budget. | Disqualify safety-certification/automatic-booking expectations and missing operational owner. |
| Demo completed | Prospect correctly explains limits and identifies a useful task. | Rehearse app/serial demo; capture comprehension and required inputs. |
| Proposal | Baseline, support cap, acceptance and commercial fields agreed in principle. | Customer-specific [pilot proposal](pilot-proposal-template.md). |
| Pilot started | Reviewed terms signed, required payment received and setup/readback checked. | [Agreement discussion template](pilot-agreement-template.md), invoice evidence and kickoff. |
| Paid continuation | Separate recurring scope signed and first continuation payment received. | Record offer/decision/payment dates and monthly fee; a verbal “yes” is not converted revenue. |

Metrics: discovery qualification = qualified farms / interviewed farms; proposal win = paid starts / proposals; sales cycle = paid-start date − first qualified date. Track cash collected separately from signed value. Use a defined mature cohort for each rate.

## Pilot-to-paid conversion playbook

1. **Before kickoff:** select one retrieval/follow-up task, baseline minutes, named operators and an agreed [ACCEPTANCE TARGET]. Quote [PILOT PRICE], support limits and separately priced exclusions.
2. **Week 1:** onboard up to two operators, create agreed batches, verify persisted readback and observation-only fallback. Label any demonstration scans synthetic; exclude them from adoption counts.
3. **Weeks 2–4:** review weekly active farm usage, timely follow-ups, missing readings, support minutes and failure reports. Document value evidence and adverse feedback, not just usage totals.
4. **Week 5:** buyer reviews baseline/current task evidence and a written [MONTHLY PRICE] offer. Identify approval and invoice steps; no silent conversion or automatic renewal.
5. **Week 6:** acceptance review and issue list. Separate workflow acceptance from any scientific accuracy discussion.
6. **Within 14 days after end:** log signed/paid continuation, decline or pending decision. Export/retention closeout follows reviewed terms if declining.

Conversion definition: farms with a signed continuation **and first payment** within 14 days / all pilot farms whose end dates are at least 14 days old. Include terminated pilots in that denominator and report reasons separately; do not hide failed pilots or count unfinished pilots as losses. A planning threshold of 50% after at least four mature pilots is a hypothesis, not statistical validation.

## Partnership terms to settle

- **Adviser:** referral permission, role in interpretation, any fee/conflict disclosure and explicit customer sharing consent.
- **Cooperative:** sponsor/payer, per-farm access, training responsibilities and no implied member-data ownership.
- **Laboratory:** sample protocol, immutable IDs, methods/basis, fees, reporting, failed-QC rules and separate research permissions; use the [MoU outline](lab-partner-mou-outline.md).
- **Device supplier:** protocol compatibility and maintenance evidence before naming a supported device; the default ESP32 sketch remains a demonstration.
