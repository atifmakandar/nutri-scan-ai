### The workflow

1. **Identify the batch:** record feed/silage identity and location.
2. **Record what you observed:** save concerns or Unknown answers without a score;
   attach authorized evidence photos.
3. **Follow up:** maintain in-app reminders and local testing-request status; a
   named person contacts the chosen laboratory/adviser outside the app.
4. **Review the record:** retrieve saved evidence and downloaded reports with
   authorized farm access. Complete screening additionally requires ten suitable
   supplied inputs and produces uncalibrated prototype outputs.

### Who should discuss a pilot?

A dairy farm with repeated batch-record or follow-up work, a named operator and an
authorized buyer. Cooperatives, advisers and laboratories may discuss introductions
or a separately defined partnership. No partnership, accreditation or cross-farm
administration capability is implied.

### Proposed pilot

A six-week, one-farm workflow pilot with agreed users, batches, onboarding/support
hours, baseline and acceptance measures. We would measure record retrieval, task
completion and delivery effort—not feed safety, scientific accuracy or guaranteed
financial outcomes. Hardware, laboratory work and research validation are separately
scoped. Pricing is supplied only after scope and professional review.

### Short FAQ

- **Does a photo test feed?** No. Photos are evidence, not AI diagnosis.
- **Does the app book a laboratory?** No. Requests/status are farm-local records.
- **Can it work offline?** Installed public help can reload offline. Private data
  needs connectivity; explicit reading drafts in an already-loaded app have limits.
- **Are the sensors validated?** Default firmware/demo uses synthetic readings;
  real sensors need suitable integration and validation.
