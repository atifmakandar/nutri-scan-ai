# Pilot agreement — non-legal discussion template

> **DRAFT — legal/accounting review required. NOT A LEGAL AGREEMENT OR READY-TO-SIGN CONTRACT.** This is a discussion checklist for qualified counsel/accountants to turn into appropriate terms. No clause is represented as sufficient or enforceable in any jurisdiction.

## 1. Parties, authority and term

- Proposed supplier: [LEGAL ENTITY], represented by [FOUNDER NAME], notice contact [CONTACT].
- Proposed customer: [CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME], authorized representative/notice contact [CUSTOMER CONTACT]. Verify signatory and payer authority in the reviewed contract.
- Pilot [PILOT ID], from [PILOT START DATE] to [PILOT END DATE]; counsel to specify effective date, addresses and notice method.
- Review owner/date: [OWNER] / [REVIEW DATE]. No automatic continuation or renewal; later paid service needs separately agreed terms.

## 2. Scope and deliverables

Attach a completed [pilot proposal](pilot-proposal-template.md) with farm/operator/batch caps, included hours, devices/browser, deployment responsibilities, dates, deliverables and explicit exclusions. Counsel must resolve order of precedence between proposal and agreement.

Current scope may include batch records, observation-only checks, reminders, farm-local testing requests, private evidence/reports and reviewed uncalibrated screening. All ten suitable inputs remain required for a full screening. Photos are evidence only. Device calibration status is a report, not verification. The prototype is not a laboratory replacement.

Exclude hardware supply/calibration, laboratory work, sample logistics, professional advice and research validation unless separately agreed. There is no automatic lab booking, toxin detection, certified calibration, diagnosis, guaranteed safety or lab accuracy commitment. No customer acceptance term may imply such a claim.

## 3. Acceptance and changes

- Attach predefined workflow tests, baseline and [ACCEPTANCE TARGET], evidence sources, review date and named reviewers. Separate observation-only use from full-screening demonstration.
- Customer records accepted deliverables, specific defects and agreed remedies in a written review. Discuss a remediation/retest period and dispute route; do not assume silence means acceptance.
- Define how partial acceptance affects payment/refunds in [PAYMENT TERMS]. Accuracy, feeding clearance or safety are not acceptance criteria.
- Both parties approve any added scope, hours, price, dates or data purpose in writing before work begins; prototype features are not an open-ended development obligation.

## 4. Data ownership, permissions and confidentiality

**Proposed allocation for counsel review:** customer retains its rights in customer-supplied farm records, photos and reports, subject to third-party rights. [LEGAL ENTITY] retains pre-existing software/methods and does not acquire farm-data ownership merely by hosting records. Resolve rights to generated reports, joint work and lab-provided data explicitly; no transfer of a laboratory's rights is implied.

Record [DATA USE PERMISSION] covering the minimum processing needed to deliver the pilot. Specify data categories, permitted purposes, controller/processor or other applicable roles, authorized staff, hosting/subprocessors, security responsibilities and incident contacts/timing. No model training, research reuse, sale, cross-customer sharing, logo use or publicity is presumed; each requires separate appropriate permission and review.

Agree confidentiality scope, recipient obligations, permitted disclosures and return/deletion handling. Farmer-selected external sharing is manual/opt-in and may create recipient-held copies. Private role controls are prototype safeguards, not audited-security/compliance claims. Do not place passwords or sensitive personal data in pipeline/KPI CSVs.

## 5. Retention, access and closeout

- Retention period: [RETENTION PERIOD]; requested export window: [EXPORT WINDOW]. Define formats, recipients, identity verification, fees (if any) and delivery responsibility.
- Current UI supports saved HTML reports, browser Print / Save PDF and individual attachment downloads. Bulk export, deletion, account deactivation and backup cleanup are **not promised self-service features**; any manual commitment needs technical confirmation, a named owner and a reviewed schedule.
- Counsel must address legal retention exceptions, backup expiry and copies already shared externally. No immediate complete-erasure promise.
- Verify farm-role access and saved readback before real use; specify customer device/network duties and supplier backup/restore duties for the agreed deployment.

## 6. Support and operating limits

Channel [CONTACT]; support window [SUPPORT HOURS]; initial response target [RESPONSE TARGET]. Define included hours, severity levels, outages, escalation, exclusions, visit costs and handling outside the support window. Response is not resolution or uptime. Make the manual fallback and reporting path explicit.

The app's public-help PWA and local drafts do not provide offline private-record access; draft save/load does not retain instrument provenance. In-app reminders do not send SMS/background notifications. Testing requests are not transmitted/booked externally and reports are not automatically interpreted. Customer remains responsible for contacting qualified professionals for feed/animal concerns.

## 7. Payment and taxes

Pilot fee [PILOT PRICE] INR; continuation, if separately agreed, [MONTHLY PRICE] INR/farm/month. Complete [PAYMENT TERMS] for deposit, milestones, invoices, due dates, disputed invoices, late/non-payment, refunds and cancellation. Complete [TAX TREATMENT] with an accountant; no tax registration/rate or gross/net treatment is asserted.

Specify who pays hardware, lab fees, transport and extra visits; include no unquoted pass-throughs. Acceptance, suspension and payment interaction must be explicit. Do not enter payment credentials in this template.

## 8. Termination and suspension

Counsel to define termination for convenience/breach, notice method and [NOTICE PERIOD], cure opportunity, non-payment suspension and any immediate suspension circumstances. Settle fees for work performed, deposits/refunds, unfinished deliverables and disputed sums in [PAYMENT TERMS].

On exit, follow agreed access, export, retention and confidentiality steps; identify a human owner for manual closeout. Do not delete customer records or promise continued access without reviewed terms and technical confirmation. Stop use of counterpart branding according to separately agreed permissions.

## 9. Warranties, liability and allocation of risk — counsel to draft

Discuss precisely what delivery commitments are appropriate for a prototype, available remedies, exclusions, mandatory rights and any insurance needs. Proposed liability-cap field: [LIABILITY CAP]. Counsel must determine basis, duration, applicable exclusions/carve-outs and whether any limit is lawful; **no liability limitation is established by this outline**.

Discuss data/confidentiality incidents, third-party claims, device/sample handling, professional reliance and any indemnities without inserting blanket waivers. No disclaimer creates permission to make unsupported safety/performance claims. Neither party should treat scoring as diagnosis, certified testing or a feeding guarantee.

Proposed [GOVERNING LAW] and [DISPUTE PROCESS] require counsel to select jurisdiction, escalation, forum and applicable formalities. Confirm representative authority, final annexes, notices, entire-agreement/change terms and signature method in a separate reviewed execution copy.
