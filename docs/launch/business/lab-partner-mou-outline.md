Discuss one or both **separate** workstreams: (a) customer-arranged testing/report follow-up, and (b) a prospective reference-data study. ## 3. Method and identity contract

Follow the [real collection protocol](../../calibration-workflow.md#real-collection-protocol-before-calibration) before reference collection:

- Specify target(s), sample types, [LAB METHOD], units, wet/dry/as-fed basis, ranges and any applicable detection/quantification limits. Verify actual method suitability and qualification evidence; do not insert invented accreditation.
- Agree [SAMPLE COUNT] based on expert study design, intended variation and independent groups; no statistically adequate sample size is supplied here.
- Assign immutable sample IDs linked to farm, batch, sample type, collection/capture time, operator, device/version, conditions and handling events. Preserve raw outputs/reports and correction history. Never join results by row order or informal description.
- Record whether device and lab aliquots came from the same homogenized material. Include receipt, storage, preparation, analysis time, QC status and replicates; agree rejection/exclusion rules **before** looking at performance.
- Keep failed-QC/contaminated samples traceable and quarantined pending expert review. Any intended contamination target requires its own method/design; this outline does not create toxin detection capability.

## 4. Study design and release gate

Pre-register target definitions, acceptance tolerances and farm/batch/time grouping. Fit preprocessing/models on train only; tune on validation; freeze before opening the locked test set. Duplicate aliquots, repeats and lab replicates must not cross split boundaries.

Report count/coverage, MAE, RMSE, signed bias, R², confidence intervals where feasible, subgroup error, repeatability and reference uncertainty. Evaluate sensitivity/specificity and false negatives when threshold decisions are intended. Seek laboratory/domain and product-safety review of any proposed release. The synthetic scaffold is not validation, its coefficients are not app-integrated, and a high R² alone authorizes no safety claim.
