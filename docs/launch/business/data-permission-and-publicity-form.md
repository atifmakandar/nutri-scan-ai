## Core delivery is a separate decision

Core pilot processing is limited to the reviewed agreement/proposal and
[privacy notice](privacy-notice-template.md): [DATA USE PERMISSION]. Record the
purpose, scope, organization roles and authority there. Refusing any optional use
below must not quietly change access to the agreed core pilot or its price.

## Optional choices — each requires its own explicit decision

No choice is preselected. For each option, record **permit / decline / not asked**,
the exact approved scope and evidence of an informed authorized decision. Do not
interpret a signature on a pilot proposal as selecting these options.

| Optional use | Initial decision | Required scope before permission can be used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research/calibration analysis | not asked | Specific dataset categories and sample IDs; methods/targets; recipients; duration; re-identification risk; withdrawal handling. |
| Model training/evaluation | not asked | Specific model/program and permitted stages; retention; lab/third-party rights; whether derived artifacts can be withdrawn and any limits. Synthetic demo metrics prove no field accuracy. |
| Named testimonial/quotation | not asked | Exact approved text, name/role, placement, channels, review/expiry and withdrawal route. |
| Farm/customer name and logo | not asked | Approved assets, purpose, channels, brand rules and publication period; signatory must hold relevant rights. |
| Photo/video or public case study | not asked | Exact approved media/draft, identifiable people, locations, results/context, channels and expiry. Separate individual permissions where needed. |
| Marketing contact | not asked | Recipient, channel, message purpose/frequency, duration and an effective opt-out process. |

Explain plain-language risks: de-identification may not prevent re-identification;
published/downloaded copies may not be recallable; deleting source rows does not
necessarily remove influence from trained artifacts. Resolve a realistic withdrawal
process **before** reuse rather than promise erasure that cannot be delivered.

## Withdrawal and publication gate

1. Verify requester authority and identify affected permissions/assets/data.
2. Stop new optional use in scope; record the request in restricted evidence.
3. Assess removal from controlled channels, recipients, datasets and artifacts with
   the responsible reviewers; explain actual limits, holds and approved timelines.
4. Record action, remaining copies/limits and the response; update permissions so
   future imports, publications or restores do not silently reinstate the use.

No approved optional choices are supplied by this pack. Permission to discuss a
pilot is not permission to publish its name, outcomes, photos, logo or contacts.
