## Recent behavior — 8 minutes

1. “Tell me about the **last** silage/feed batch you checked. What happened from receiving/opening it to the next action?”
2. “Who wrote down observations? Where could someone else find them later? Can you walk through the last handoff without sharing private data?”
3. “When did a missing reading, report or follow-up last cause extra work? What did you do, and roughly how much time did it take?” Do not suggest it caused animal harm or loss.
4. “What did you do when something looked concerning or you were unsure? Who decided whether to test?”
5. “How was the last laboratory test arranged, paid for and matched back to its batch? What went wrong or worked well?”
6. “Which measurements can you actually obtain, from which instruments/reports and with what units/basis? Which are unavailable?”
7. “How often does this workflow occur? How many batches/operators are involved, and when is it busiest?”

Ask for recent instances, not agreement with a sales claim. Record exact quotes only with permission, and record “no problem” responses as carefully as problems.

## Alternatives, purchasing and constraints — 5 minutes

- “What notebook, spreadsheet, software, adviser or service do you use now? What do you like about it?”
- “What did you spend on the workflow or testing recently? Which part was software, laboratory work, labor or travel?” Accept “unknown”; do not estimate for them.
- “Who would use a new tool, approve it and pay its invoice? What approval steps and timing apply?”
- “What would prevent adoption: phone/browser, language, connectivity, effort, trust, privacy or something else?” Ask open-ended first, then probe.
- “What data could be shared with a provider, and what must stay private? Who can authorize that?”

## Concept check and pilot offer — 5 minutes

Only after the behavior questions, explain: “Nutri-Scan currently organizes batch observations, reminders and farm-local testing/report records. It also demonstrates reviewed, uncalibrated screening; photos are evidence only and testing requests are not booked automatically.”

1. “Which, if any, step would be useful in your current routine? What would you remove?”
2. “What do you understand the score, photo and testing-request features **cannot** establish?” Correct any inference of lab accuracy, toxin detection, certified calibration, diagnosis or guaranteed safety using the [claims matrix](README.md#claims-matrix).
3. “If you lacked the required measurements, would observation-only recording still help? Why or why not?” Do not propose guessed readings.
4. “Would you consider a six-week, one-farm workflow pilot at [PILOT PRICE], excluding hardware/lab work? What task and result would justify payment? Who would need to approve it?” Only use a completed, reviewed price in a live offer.
5. “What evidence would justify a separate [MONTHLY PRICE] continuation? May we schedule a decision with the buyer?” Distinguish stated interest from a signed scope/payment.
