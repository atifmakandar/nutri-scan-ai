## 1. Customer problem and baseline

Choose one task, for example retrieving the latest batch report and identifying the pending follow-up. ## 2. Proposed scope

Planning example: six weeks, one farm account, up to two operators and ten managed silage batches; four onboarding hours and two follow-up/support hours. Replace with the agreed dates, caps and responsibilities before quoting.

Included activities:

- Set up agreed farm roles and batch identities; train users on observations, private evidence and in-app follow-up.
- Track manually arranged testing requests and store/download trusted customer-supplied reports.
- Demonstrate reviewed serial/manual input and uncalibrated scoring **only with all ten suitable inputs**. Keep synthetic training records separate from real workflow records and exclude them from KPIs.
- Review weekly usage, task timing, follow-up completion, missing inputs and delivery effort.
- Provide a final outcome review and the agreed export/closeout assistance.

**Excluded unless separately scoped:** hardware supply/validation, sensor calibration, laboratory fees/sample logistics, scientific reference study, multi-farm dashboard, SMS, automatic lab booking, OCR/report interpretation, AI image analysis and professional feeding advice. No lab accuracy, toxin detection, certified calibration, diagnosis or guaranteed safety.

## 4. Success and acceptance

Set [ACCEPTANCE TARGET] jointly before kickoff. Illustrative candidates: weekly use in four of six weeks; 80% of agreed follow-ups completed by their due dates; 20% lower median retrieval time across at least five comparable attempts. These are **proposed workflow targets**, not promised improvements or scientific validation.

| Measure | Definition / evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly active farm | At least one agreed non-demo observation, managed-batch update or follow-up status change in a week. |
| Follow-up completion | Tasks completed on/before due date / all agreed tasks due in the period; include missed tasks. |
| Retrieval change | `(baseline median − current median) / baseline median`; same task, comparable attempts, positive baseline; report sample count and exceptions. Current value: [CURRENT MINUTES]. |
| Workflow integrity | Saved identity/readback, Unknown preserved, role access and request/report progression checked on the agreed setup. |
| Delivery load | Onboarding/support/visit hours and out-of-scope requests logged separately. |

Use the [KPI log](../templates/business/pilot-kpi-log.csv). A pilot with no full screenings can still meet agreed observation/follow-up goals; do not report it as evidence of scoring performance.
