## Presenter preparation

- Attach an evidence date to every number. Keep proposed KPIs and observed results visibly distinct.
- Build market sizing bottom-up only after discovering reachable farms, qualification rates and an evidenced price: reachable qualified farms × tested annual fee. Do not present the product as serving every dairy farmer today.
- Carry a limitations slide/appendix using the [claims matrix](README.md#claims-matrix). No lab accuracy, toxin detection, certified calibration, diagnosis, guaranteed safety or automatic lab booking claim.
- Have counsel review the fundraising communication and any customer/data references; have an accountant reconcile the financial model. Use placeholders until identity, budget and terms are verified.
