## Entity, authority and finance

- [ ] Decide the legal operating/contracting entity with counsel/accountant; verify
  founders, ownership, authority and any institutional/employment restrictions.
- [ ] Confirm whether the project name can be used; investigate relevant company,
  domain and trademark conflicts before public expenditure. No trademark is claimed.
- [ ] Verify required entity/tax/local registrations for actual activities and
  territory. Do not assume startup recognition, incentives or tax treatment applies.
- [ ] Establish authorized invoicing, payment collection, expense approval,
  bookkeeping, refunds and reconciliation; protect account/payment credentials.
- [ ] Document founder contributions, equity/vesting, decision rights, departures,
  related-party arrangements and signing/spending authority appropriately.
- [ ] Review budgets and cash timing; treat grants, investments and partner support
  as zero committed funding until execution and actual availability are verified.

## Ownership and third-party rights

- [ ] Inventory source code, presentations, artwork, firmware, datasets, methods and
  commissioned work; identify authors and provenance without assuming assignment.
- [ ] Review SIH/institution/employer/sponsor rules, submission terms and contributor
  agreements; resolve ownership and commercialization authority in writing.
- [ ] Review open-source licences/notices and third-party media/data terms; keep a
  dependency/licence inventory for the delivered release. No licence audit is implied.
- [ ] Obtain appropriate written rights for contractors, joint research, lab reports,
  training/reference data and generated material. Farm authorization may not cover
  a lab's or an individual's rights.
- [ ] Separate customer-owned records from supplier pre-existing software and later
  research. No exclusive dataset, patent, accuracy moat or proprietary model is
  claimed without evidence and rights review.
- [ ] Use the [permission record](data-permission-and-publicity-form.md) for any
  optional research, testimonial, customer logo or public case study.

## Product activity, safety and insurance

- [ ] Describe actual intended use to a qualified adviser: workflow software versus
  measurement hardware, laboratory services, advisory or another regulated activity.
  Determine applicable obligations before supply; a disclaimer is not an exemption.
- [ ] If supplying/importing/assembling hardware, review applicable electrical,
  radio, battery, environmental, warranty and distribution requirements for the
  exact product and territory. Do not imply certification from a board's branding.
- [ ] If handling physical samples or providing tests, confirm provider qualification,
  methods, handling/transport, chain of custody, reporting and relevant permissions.
- [ ] Review professional/product/public liability, cyber and equipment insurance
  needs with a qualified broker; record coverage and exclusions, not assumed cover.
- [ ] Align contracts, demonstrations, translations and marketing with the current
  [claims matrix](README.md#claims-matrix); expert review is separate from sales approval.

## Data, contracts and operations

- [ ] Approve the [privacy notice](privacy-notice-template.md), organization roles,
  retention, provider terms, access withdrawal, deletion and incident processes.
- [ ] Obtain reviewed pilot/continuation terms, proposal, quotation, support scope,
  tax treatment, refund/termination and dispute procedures before charging.
- [ ] Complete [technical readiness](../technical/technical-readiness-checklist.md),
  restore/privacy tests and scoped release approval before real customer data.
- [ ] Assign accountable support, finance, domain, hardware and data owners; one
  founder may hold several roles but each responsibility still needs coverage.
